Wuwen Symbol

Concept

¿w? is the visual symbol of Wuwen.

It is not merely a logo.

It is a visual expression of the fundamental philosophy of Wuwen:

«Do not begin with an answer. Begin with a question.»

The symbol represents an honest runtime that asks, checks, corresponds, and corrects before making a claim.

---

Symbol Structure

The symbol is based on the complete Spanish question mark:

¿w?

The ¿ and ? form the outer structure.

The lowercase w is placed within the question-mark form.

The question mark remains the primary visual element.

The lowercase w is intentional.

It represents the smallest visible unit of Wuwen identity:

- ¿w? — visual symbol
- W — protocol and technical identity
- Wuwen（勿问） — public brand identity
- WAL — formal protocol name

The lowercase w therefore belongs to the symbol itself and should not be replaced by an uppercase W.

---

The Question Mark

The complete Spanish question mark represents inquiry.

It does not represent uncertainty as a weakness.

It represents the discipline of refusing to claim more than the available evidence can support.

The symbol begins with:

«¿»

and ends with:

«?»

The question therefore surrounds the runtime.

Wuwen does not begin by asserting an answer.

Wuwen begins by asking:

«What is being claimed?»

---

The Meaning of w

The lowercase w is the minimal identity mark of Wuwen.

It can be understood through the Wuwen semantic structure:

- Witness
- Unknown
- What
- Evidence
- No unsupported claim

The lowercase form emphasizes that the symbol is not a declaration of authority.

It is a compact sign of a process:

«Question → Evidence → Correspondence → Responsibility»

The w is therefore not an abbreviation of a former name.

It is the minimal visual expression of Wuwen itself.

---

Continuous Runtime

The inner w may be rendered with smooth, continuous curves.

The curved form represents:

- continuous runtime;
- continuous correspondence;
- continuous verification;
- continuous correction.

The visual form should communicate continuity rather than finality.

Wuwen is not a machine that produces a final answer and stops.

It is a runtime that remains capable of checking whether a claim is supported by its evidence and responsibility boundary.

A new question may reopen correspondence, verification, evidence, or correction.

Therefore:

«Wuwen is not a final judgment.»

«Wuwen is a continuous process of honest correspondence.»

---

Visual Principles

The symbol should follow these principles:

1. Preserve the complete Spanish question-mark structure.
2. Keep the lowercase w as the internal identity element.
3. Keep the w visually subordinate to the question mark.
4. Preserve the recognizable form of w.
5. Smooth or curved forms may be used to express continuity.
6. Keep the composition clean and minimal.
7. Do not introduce M into the current Wuwen identity.
8. Do not use the symbol to imply authority or final judgment.
9. The symbol should communicate question before conclusion.
10. The symbol should communicate honesty before assertion.
