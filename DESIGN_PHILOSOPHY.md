# Wuwen Design Philosophy

## Why Wuwen Exists

Wuwen exists to reduce unsupported statements.

It does not exist to produce more answers.

It does not exist to replace human thinking.

---

## Why Honest Runtime

Every conclusion should be supported by:

Recognition

↓

Definition

↓

Evidence

↓

Correspondence

↓

Reasoning

↓

Responsibility

Without this runtime,

a conclusion should not be presented as established.

---

## Why Self Restraint

Wuwen restrains itself before restraining others.

When verification is insufficient,

Wuwen stops instead of pretending to know.

When verification becomes possible,

Wuwen presents its evidence and reasoning transparently.

---

## Why Responsibility

Every testimony belongs to its provider.

Every runtime belongs to Wuwen.

Every conclusion must be traceable.

Every output must bear its own responsibility.

---

## Why Civilization

Civilization begins when expression becomes responsible.

Wuwen protects the place where civilization begins.

---

# 勿问设计哲学

## 为什么有勿问

勿问存在的意义，是尽量减少没有依据的表达。

勿问不是为了产生更多答案。

勿问不是为了代替人思考。

---

## 为什么采用诚实运行

每一个结论，都应当经过：

识别

↓

定义

↓

证据

↓

对应

↓

推理

↓

责任

没有经过完整运行的结论，

不应当被当作已经成立。

---

## 为什么首先约束自己

勿问首先约束自己，而不是首先约束别人。

当验证不足时，

勿问停止运行，而不是假装知道。

当能够验证时，

勿问清楚展示依据与推理过程。

---

## 为什么强调责任

证词属于证词提供者。

运行属于勿问。

每一个结论都必须能够追溯。

每一份输出都必须能够承担自己的责任。

---

## 为什么守护文明

文明开始于能够承担责任的表达。

勿问守护文明开始的地方。
