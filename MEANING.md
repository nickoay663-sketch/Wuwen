# Wuwen Meaning

# 勿问意义

## 1 · Definition

Meaning is the understanding reconstructed from an expression.

Meaning is not directly observable.

Meaning exists through expressions.

意义，是从表达中重建出来的理解。

意义不能被直接观察。

意义存在于表达之中。

---

## 2 · Relationship

Meaning does not exist independently.

Meaning is carried by expressions.

Without expression, meaning cannot enter Honest Runtime.

意义不是独立存在的对象。

意义由表达承载。

没有表达，意义无法进入诚实运行。

---

## 3 · Recognition

Recognition does not directly observe meaning.

Recognition reconstructs meaning by analyzing expressions and their relationships.

Recognition cannot read thoughts.

Recognition only reconstructs the meaning carried by expressions.

Recognition 不直接观察意义。

Recognition 通过分析表达及其关联关系重建意义。

Recognition 不能读取思想。

Recognition 只能重建表达所承载的意义。

---

## 4 · Honest Runtime

Honest Runtime does not evaluate carriers.

Honest Runtime evaluates the reconstructed meaning of expressions.

诚实运行不检验载体。

诚实运行检验表达重建后的意义。

---

## 5 · Principle

Different carriers may express the same meaning.

The same carrier may express different meanings under different contexts.

Therefore, meaning shall always be reconstructed before entering Honest Runtime.

不同载体可以表达相同意义。

相同载体在不同语境下可以表达不同意义。

因此，意义必须先经过重建，再进入诚实运行。

## 7 · Purpose

The purpose of Meaning Reconstruction is to enable Honest Runtime to examine what an expression actually conveys rather than merely what its carrier contains.

意义重建的目的，是使诚实运行检验表达真正传达的意义，而不是仅检验载体本身包含的信息。
