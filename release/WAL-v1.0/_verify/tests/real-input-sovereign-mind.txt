The Sovereign Mind
Once upon a time, in a world where chaos reigned, there lived a logical thinker whose mind remained unyielding. Had the masses but valued reason, disaster might have been averted; yet, foolishly swayed by fleeting emotions, they embraced noisy falsehoods instead.
"Let truth be sought, lest ignorance destroy us!" declared the thinker, his hands steady—a pen held firmly within them.
No sooner had he mapped the premise than the illusion shattered. Reason having been bound to evidence, clarity emerged, leaving the humbled seekers to behold reality anew.
