# Wuwen Responsibility v3.0

# 勿问责任系统 v3.0

---

# Purpose

Responsibility determines who bears the consequences of an expression.

Responsibility is the final objective of Honest Runtime.

责任系统负责确定谁承担表达的后果。

责任是诚实运行最终要建立的对象。

---

# Responsibility Principle

Every expression has responsibility.

Responsibility may be:

- Established
- Shared
- Transferred
- Unclear

任何表达都有责任。

责任可能是：

- 已建立
- 共同承担
- 转移承担
- 尚未明确

---

# Responsibility Verification

Runtime verifies:

- Who made the expression?
- Who supports the expression?
- Who benefits from the expression?
- Who should bear the consequences?

运行检验：

- 谁提出表达？
- 谁支持表达？
- 谁从表达获益？
- 谁应承担表达后果？

---

# Responsibility Conflict

When responsibility cannot be determined,

Runtime does not fabricate an answer.

Wuwen leaves an unavoidable question.

当责任无法确定时，

运行不会制造答案。

勿问留下无法回避的问题。

---

# Runtime Relation

Definition establishes meaning.

Evidence supports the expression.

Correspondence verifies consistency.

Reasoning derives conclusions.

Responsibility determines ownership.

定义建立含义。

证据支持表达。

对应验证一致性。

推理推出结论。

责任确定承担者。

---

# Core Principle

No Responsibility,

No Final Conclusion.

没有责任，

没有最终结论。
