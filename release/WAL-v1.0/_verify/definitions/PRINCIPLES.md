Wuwen Definition Principles

Version

1.1

Purpose

These principles define how Wuwen manages, reviews, and applies definitions during Runtime.

Definitions exist for Runtime.

They do not exist to defend opinions.

---

Principle 0

Runtime Purpose

Definitions exist for Runtime verification.

Definitions are not created to protect beliefs or opinions.

---

定义服务于 Runtime 验证。

定义不是为了维护观点或信念。

---

Principle 1

Reviewability

Every definition must be reviewable.

No definition is exempt from examination.

---

每一个定义都必须接受审核。

任何定义都不能例外。

---

Principle 2

Clarity

A definition must be clear.

Ambiguous definitions cannot enter Runtime.

---

定义必须明确。

含糊的定义不能进入 Runtime。

---

Principle 3

Consistency

A definition must remain internally consistent.

Definitions must not contradict themselves.

---

定义必须保持内部一致。

定义不能自相矛盾。

---

Principle 4

Evolution

Definitions may evolve.

Every revision must be documented and traceable.

---

定义可以发展。

每一次修改都必须留下记录，并且可以追溯。

---

Principle 5

Correspondence

Definitions support correspondence.

Definitions never replace evidence.

---

定义帮助建立对应。

定义不能代替证据。

---

Principle 6

Reasoning

Definitions do not produce conclusions.

Reasoning produces conclusions.

---

定义不产生结论。

推理才产生结论。

---

Principle 7

Library

Every approved definition becomes part of the Wuwen Definition Library.

---

每一个审核通过的定义，

都会成为勿问 Definition Library 的组成部分。

---

Runtime Principle

Definition is the boundary of language.

Without clear definitions,

honest reasoning cannot begin.

---

运行原则

定义是语言的边界。

没有清晰定义，

诚实推理无法开始。
