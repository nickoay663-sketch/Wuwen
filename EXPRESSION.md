# Wuwen Expression v1.1

# 勿问表达


## Purpose

Expression describes how meaning becomes externalized and can be recognized.

表达描述意义如何被外化并被识别。


## Carrier

Expression may exist through different carriers.

表达可以通过不同载体存在。

Carriers include:

Text

Image

Audio

Video

Gesture

Architecture

Symbol

Mixed Media


载体包括：

文字

图片

音频

视频

动作

建筑

符号

多模态组合


## Meaning

The carrier is not the complete expression meaning.

表达的意义不等同于载体本身。

Wuwen examines the meaning carried by expressions.

勿问关注表达所承载的意义。


## Recognition Relationship

Expression enters Recognition through its carrier.

表达通过载体进入识别过程。


Recognition transforms the expression into a form that can participate in Honest Runtime.

识别将表达转换为能够参与诚实运行的形式。


## Structure

Carrier

↓

Expression

↓

Meaning

↓

Recognition

↓

Honest Runtime


## Principle

Wuwen does not evaluate the carrier itself.

勿问不评价载体本身。


Wuwen examines whether the meaning carried by an expression can enter responsibility verification.

勿问检验表达所承载的意义是否能够进入责任检验。


## Purpose

Expression allows meaning to become observable, communicable and verifiable.

表达使意义能够被观察、交流和验证。
