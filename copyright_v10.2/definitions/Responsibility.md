Responsibility

Definition

Responsibility is the obligation to answer for one's own testimony, actions, and conclusions.

Responsibility cannot be transferred merely by assertion.

Responsibility follows the provider of testimony.

Wuwen does not assume responsibility for testimony.

Wuwen performs honest runtime.

Responsibility remains with the testimony provider.

Required Conditions

Responsibility requires:

- A testimony
- A testimony provider
- Traceable reasoning
- Traceable conclusions

Without traceability,

responsibility cannot be determined.

Responsibility

概念

责任

定义

责任，是对自己的证词、行为与结论承担回应义务。

责任不能仅凭声明而转移。

责任始终跟随证词提供者。

运行意义

勿问不承担证词本身的责任。

勿问执行诚实运行。

责任始终属于证词提供者。

所需条件

责任需要：

- 存在证词
- 存在证词提供者
- 推理可以追溯
- 结论可以追溯

如果不能追溯，

责任就无法确定。

责任

每一个人，都对自己的证词负责。

勿问只对自己的运行过程负责。

运行原则

责任跟随证词。

责任不能脱离证据而转移。

诚实运行不能代替人的责任。
