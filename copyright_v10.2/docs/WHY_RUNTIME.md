# Why Honest Runtime Exists

## Status

Research Reconstruction Draft

---

# Why Runtime?

Wuwen does not begin with conclusions.

Wuwen begins with expressions.

勿问不是从结论开始。

勿问从表达开始。

Every expression enters Honest Runtime.

所有表达进入诚实运行。

Because an expression cannot directly assume its own responsibility.

因为表达不能直接承担自己的责任。

It must first know what it is.

表达必须首先知道自己是什么。

---

# Why Recognition?

Recognition identifies the object.

识别负责识别对象。

Without recognition,

Runtime does not know what enters the system.

没有识别，

运行系统不知道进入的是什么。

---

# Why Definition?

Definition determines identity.

定义决定身份。

Without Definition,

one expression may pretend to be another.

没有定义，

一种表达便可能冒充另一种表达。

Definition is therefore the entrance of Honest Runtime.

因此，

定义是诚实运行的入口。

---

# Why Search?

Search looks for existing knowledge.

检索寻找已有知识。

Runtime does not create knowledge.

Runtime first discovers knowledge.

运行系统不创造知识。

运行系统首先发现知识。

---

# Why Evidence?

Evidence examines testimony.

证据检验证词。

Without testimony,

every conclusion becomes unsupported.

没有证词，

任何结论都会失去支撑。

---

# Why Correspondence?

Correspondence examines whether expressions correspond to their objects.

对应检验表达是否真正对应对象。

Names must correspond to objects.

Definitions must correspond to concepts.

Evidence must correspond to testimony.

Conclusion must correspond to reasoning.

名称必须对应对象。

定义必须对应概念。

证词必须对应证词对象。

结论必须对应推理。

---

# Why Reasoning?

Reasoning examines whether conclusions can be derived.

推理检验结论是否能够推出。

Wuwen does not generate conclusions.

Wuwen verifies reasoning.

勿问不制造结论。

勿问检验推理。

---

# Why Responsibility?

Every expression finally assumes Expression Responsibility.

所有表达最终承担表达责任。

Responsibility identifies who expressed,

what was expressed,

how it was reasoned,

and what must therefore be assumed.

责任识别：

谁表达、

表达什么、

如何推导、

因此承担什么。

---

# Why Reconstruction?

Reconstruction does not repair expressions.

Reconstruction restores expressions.

重构不是修补表达。

重构恢复表达。

Expressions return to their proper identities.

表达回到自己的身份。

Expressions therefore better assume their responsibilities.

表达因此更能够承担自己的责任。

---

# Why SelfCheck?

Wuwen itself is also an expression.

勿问自身也是表达。

Therefore,

Wuwen itself must enter the same Honest Runtime.

因此，

勿问自身也必须进入同一套诚实运行。

SelfCheck guarantees that Runtime itself remains honest.

自检保证运行系统自身保持诚实。

---

# Honest Runtime

Recognition.

Definition.

Search.

Evidence.

Correspondence.

Reasoning.

Responsibility.

Reconstruction.

SelfCheck.

These are not independent modules.

They are one continuous Honest Runtime.

识别。

定义。

检索。

证据。

对应。

推理。

责任。

重构。

自检。

它们不是独立模块。

它们共同组成一套连续运行的诚实运行系统。

---

Returning every expression to honesty,

allows every expression to assume responsibility.

When expressions assume responsibility,

civilization continues.

让表达回到诚实。

让表达承担责任。

文明因此得以持续。
