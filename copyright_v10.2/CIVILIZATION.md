# Wuwen Civilization

# 勿问文明

## 1 · Definition

Civilization is sustained through expressions.

A civilization can continue only when expressions are able to communicate truthfully and assume responsibility.

文明通过表达持续运行。

只有当表达能够诚实交流并承担责任时，文明才能持续运行。

---

## 2 · Honest Beginning

Honesty is the beginning of civilization.

Without honesty, expressions lose trust.

Without trust, civilization cannot continue.

诚实是文明开始的地方。

没有诚实，表达失去信任。

没有信任，文明无法持续。

---

## 3 · Expression

Civilization operates through expressions.

Law, education, science, culture, commerce, media and public communication all rely on expressions.

文明通过表达运行。

法律、教育、科学、文化、商业、媒体以及公共交流，都依赖表达运行。

---

## 4 · Honest Runtime Standard

Wuwen does not require identical viewpoints.

Wuwen requires every expression to enter the same Honest Runtime.

Different definitions or evidence may lead to different conclusions.

The Honest Runtime Standard remains the same.

勿问不要求观点一致。

勿问要求所有表达进入同一套诚实运行。

不同定义、不同证据可以得到不同结论。

诚实运行标准始终一致。

---

## 5 · Civilization Standard

The Honest Runtime Standard is the Civilization Standard of Wuwen.

Civilization is not defined by identical conclusions.

Civilization is defined by whether every expression accepts the same Honest Runtime Standard.

同一套诚实运行标准，就是勿问的文明标准。

文明不是结论一致。

文明是所有表达接受同一套诚实运行标准。

Wuwen exists to help expressions return to honesty.

When expressions are reconstructed into forms that can better assume responsibility, civilization continues through them.

勿问存在的目的，是帮助表达回到诚实。

当表达被重构为更能够承担责任的表达，文明便通过这些表达持续运行。
