## Expression（表达）

Expression is identifiable information formed in relation to an object.

表达，是针对对象形成的可识别信息。


## Language Environment（语言环境）

Language Environment is the language condition, rules and context in which an expression exists and is understood.

语言环境，是表达存在和被理解时所处的语言条件、规则和语境。


## Semantic Object（统一语义对象）

Semantic Object is the structured object created from an expression after recognition.

统一语义对象，是表达经过识别后形成的结构化对象。


## Object（对象）

Object is the entity, concept or target referred to by an expression.

对象，是表达所对应的事物、概念或目标。


## Testimony（证词）

Testimony is an expression with an identifiable source entering Honest Runtime.

证词，是具有可识别来源并进入诚实运行的表达。


## Definition（定义）

Definition is the process of determining the meaning of an object.

定义，是确定对象含义的过程。


## Evidence（证据）

Evidence is information with a source used to verify whether testimony has support.

证据，是具有来源并用于验证证词是否具有支持的信息。


## Correspondence（对应）

Correspondence is the process of checking relationships between expression, definition, object and evidence.

对应，是检验表达、定义、对象和证据之间关系的过程。


## Reasoning（推理）

Reasoning is the process of deriving conclusions from verified relationships.

推理，是根据已经验证的关系形成结论的过程。


## Conclusion（结论）

Conclusion is a result formed from expressions and reasoning.

结论，是由表达和推理形成的结果。


## Responsibility（责任）

Responsibility is the relationship between the source of an expression and the result produced by that expression.

责任，是表达来源与表达产生结果之间的承担关系。


## Reconstruction（重构）

Reconstruction is the process of producing an expression that can better bear responsibility after Runtime verification.

重构，是在运行验证完成后生成更能承担责任的表达的过程。


## Question（问题）

Question is an expression requesting verification of another expression.

问题，是请求验证某个表达的表达。


## Claim（主张）

Claim is an expression requesting recognition or acceptance.

主张，是要求被认可或接受的表达。


## Meaning（意义）

Meaning is the understanding relationship formed between an expression and an object.

意义，是表达与对象之间形成的理解关系。
