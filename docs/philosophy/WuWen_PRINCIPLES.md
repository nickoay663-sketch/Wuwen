# Wuwen Principles v1.0

# 勿问原则 v1.0


## Foundation

Wuwen Principles are derived from Wuwen First Axiom.

勿问原则来源于勿问第一公理。


> 主体不可互相否定。


Wuwen does not judge the value of a person.

Wuwen verifies whether expressions, actions and systems preserve the conditions for subjects to exist, understand reality, make choices and assume responsibility.


勿问不判断人的价值。

勿问检验表达、行为和系统是否维护主体存在、认识现实、进行选择和承担责任的条件。


---

# Principle 1

## Subject Principle

## 主体原则


Every expression must first identify the subjects involved.

任何表达首先必须识别其中涉及的主体。


A subject has:

主体具有：

- Existence
- Recognition
- Choice
- Responsibility


- 存在
- 认识
- 选择
- 责任


Any process that removes these conditions from another subject is subject denial.


任何使另一个主体失去这些条件的过程，属于主体否定。


---

# Principle 2

## Truth Principle

## 真实原则


A subject must be able to understand reality through authentic information.


主体必须能够通过真实信息认识现实。


Therefore:


因此：


False information, intentional concealment of critical facts, or manipulation of judgment damages the subject's ability to understand reality.


虚假信息、故意隐藏关键事实、操纵判断能力，会破坏主体认识现实的能力。


Honesty is the mechanism that preserves truthful connection between subjects.


诚实是维护主体之间真实连接的机制。


---

# Principle 3

## Autonomy Principle

## 自主原则


A subject must be able to make choices based on authentic understanding.


主体必须能够基于真实认识进行选择。


Replacing another subject's judgment without valid authorization is subject denial.


未经有效授权替代另一个主体判断，属于主体否定。


---

# Principle 4

## Responsibility Principle

## 责任原则


A subject who makes a choice must correspondingly bear the consequences of that choice.


进行选择的主体，必须对应承担该选择产生的后果。


Separation between action and consequence damages subject boundaries.


行为与后果分离，会破坏主体边界。


Responsibility restores correspondence between subject and consequence.


责任恢复主体与后果之间的对应关系。


---

# Principle 5

## Boundary Principle

## 边界原则


Multiple subjects can coexist only when each subject has recognized boundaries.


多个主体只有在相互承认边界的条件下才能共同存在。


Arbitrary invasion of another subject's boundary is subject denial.


任意侵犯另一个主体边界，属于主体否定。


---

# Principle 6

## Fairness Principle

## 公平原则


Rules between subjects must not deny the subject status of any participant.


主体之间的规则不能否定任何参与者的主体资格。


A rule must be applicable consistently to all subjects under the same conditions.


同等条件下，规则必须能够适用于所有主体。

# Principle 8

## Self-Check Principle

## 自检原则


Wuwen Runtime must verify itself.


勿问运行系统必须验证自身。


No Wuwen conclusion may be established by denying the subject.


任何勿问结论，都不得通过否定主体来成立。

# Final Definition


文明 is the condition where multiple subjects achieve stable coexistence without denying each other's subjectivity.


文明，是多个主体在不互相否定主体性的条件下，实现稳定共同存在的状态。
