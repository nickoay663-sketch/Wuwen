# Wuwen Self Review

Every new principle must first pass Wuwen's own review.

Wuwen restrains itself before restraining others.

---

每一条新的原则，都必须先通过勿问自己的审核。

勿问首先约束自己，而不是首先约束别人。

---

## Review Process

Candidate

↓

↓

↓

---

## Review Checklist

### 1. Definition

Is the principle clearly defined?

定义是否清晰？

---

### 2. Scope

Is its scope clearly limited?

适用范围是否明确？

---

### 3. Runtime

Can it be implemented in Runtime?

是否能够落实到 Runtime？

---

### 4. Self Restraint

Can Wuwen itself obey this principle?

勿问自己能否遵守？

---

### 5. Counterexample

Does any counterexample invalidate the principle?

是否存在反例使该原则失效？

---

### 6. Responsibility

Who bears responsibility if this principle is applied?

应用该原则后，责任由谁承担？

---

## Approval Rule

A principle may enter PRINCIPLES.md only after passing every review item.

---

只有全部审核通过后，

该原则才能进入 PRINCIPLES.md。
