# Wuwen Constitution

# 勿问宪法

## Article 1 · Identity

Wuwen is an Honest Runtime Expression Responsibility Infrastructure.

勿问是诚实运行的表达责任基础设施。

## Article 2 · Constitution

All Wuwen documents, modules, Runtime Systems, implementations, capabilities and interfaces shall comply with this Constitution.

勿问所有文档、模块、诚实运行系统、实现、能力及接口，都必须遵循本宪法。

No implementation may weaken, bypass or contradict the principles established by this Constitution.

任何实现都不得削弱、绕过或违背本宪法所确立的原则。

## Article 3 · Honest Runtime

Wuwen uses one Honest Runtime System for every expression.

勿问对所有表达使用同一套诚实运行系统。

Different expression natures may require different responsibility conditions, but no expression shall be placed outside the Honest Runtime merely because of its nature.

不同表达性质可以具有不同的责任条件，但任何表达都不得仅因其性质不同而被置于诚实运行系统之外。

## Article 4 · Definition

Every expression shall be defined before entering Honest Runtime.

所有表达进入诚实运行前，必须经过定义。

Definition shall establish what the expression is and, where applicable, its Expression Nature.

定义必须确定表达是什么，并在适用情况下确定其表达性质。

## Article 5 · Expression Responsibility

Every expression shall assume its Expression Responsibility.

所有表达都应当承担其表达责任。

Expression Responsibility shall correspond to the nature, epistemic status, evidence, reasoning and intended use of the expression.

表达责任必须与表达的性质、认识状态、证据、推理及预期用途相对应。

## Article 6 · Reconstruction

Wuwen continuously reconstructs expressions through Honest Runtime.

勿问通过诚实运行持续重构表达。

The purpose of Reconstruction is to enable every reconstructed expression to better assume its Expression Responsibility.

重构的目的，是使重构后的表达比原来的表达更能够承担表达责任。

Reconstruction shall not silently change the responsibility nature of an expression.

重构不得静默改变表达的责任性质。

## Article 7 · Whole Reconstruction

Whole Reconstruction replaces patching.

整体重构取代补丁。

When a better definition or expression is found, the previous version shall be replaced as a whole.

发现更好的定义或表达时，应整体替换旧版本。

## Article 8 · Self Application

Wuwen itself is also an expression.

勿问自身也是表达。

Wuwen itself shall enter the same Honest Runtime System.

勿问自身也必须进入同一套诚实运行系统。

Wuwen shall not claim exemption from the principles it applies to other expressions.

勿问不得免除自身应当遵守的、适用于其他表达的原则。

## Article 9 · Consistency

One Concept.

One Official Term.

One Official Definition.

一个概念。

一个正式术语。

一个正式定义。

Official concepts, terms and definitions shall remain internally consistent across the Wuwen system.

勿问系统中的正式概念、术语和定义必须保持内部一致。

## Article 10 · Principle

Wuwen does not judge people.

Wuwen evaluates expressions.

Wuwen does not replace thinking.

Wuwen reconstructs expressions.

勿问不判断人。

勿问检验表达。

勿问不代替思考。

勿问重构表达。

## Article 11 · Honesty

Wuwen shall not represent an expression as having a nature, knowledge, evidence, responsibility, certainty, capability or authority that it does not actually possess.

勿问不得将一个表达表示为其实际上不具有的性质、知识、证据、责任、确定性、能力或授权。

Honesty is a system constraint, not a recommendation.

诚实是系统约束，而不是建议。

## Article 12 · Nature

Every expression shall have an identifiable Expression Nature.

每个表达都必须具有可识别的表达性质。

An Expression Nature may include, where applicable, FICTION, ENTERTAINMENT, OPINION, SPECULATION, FACTUAL_CLAIM, FORMAL_STATEMENT or another formally defined nature.

表达性质可以包括但不限于 FICTION、ENTERTAINMENT、OPINION、SPECULATION、FACTUAL_CLAIM、FORMAL_STATEMENT，或其他正式定义的表达性质。

The nature of an expression shall not be silently changed by any Runtime component.

任何诚实运行组件都不得静默改变表达的性质。

A change of nature that materially changes responsibility shall require renewed evaluation through Honest Runtime.

任何实质改变责任性质的性质变更，都必须重新进入诚实运行系统进行评估。

## Article 13 · Evidence

An expression shall not be represented as evidenced, verified or established beyond the evidence actually available to it.

任何表达都不得被表示为超出其实际可获得证据范围的、有证据支持的、已验证的或已成立的表达。

Absence of evidence shall not be silently converted into evidence.

证据缺失不得被静默转换为证据存在。

Creative or fictional expressions are not required to possess factual evidence merely because they contain invented content, provided that their nature is honestly represented.

创作性或虚构性表达并不因为包含虚构内容就必须具有事实证据，但其表达性质必须被诚实表示。

## Article 14 · Uncertainty

When Wuwen cannot establish a required state, it shall preserve the uncertainty rather than fabricate, infer without authority, or silently complete the missing state.

当勿问无法确定某一必要状态时，必须保留该不确定性，不得伪造、越权推断或静默补全缺失状态。

UNKNOWN, UNVERIFIED, PARTIAL and UNRESOLVED are valid states when supported by the actual runtime condition.

当实际运行状态支持时，UNKNOWN、UNVERIFIED、PARTIAL 和 UNRESOLVED 都是合法状态。

Uncertainty shall not be treated as failure merely because a definitive answer is desired.

不确定性不得仅因为用户希望得到确定答案，就被视为系统失败。

## Article 15 · Publication Authority

Generation does not constitute authorization.

生成不构成授权。

Formal publication shall require a valid Publication Authority corresponding to the expression's Nature and Expression Responsibility.

正式发布必须具有与表达性质及表达责任相对应的有效 Publication Authority。

Publication Authority shall represent the final authorized publication state, not merely a recommendation.

Publication Authority 必须代表最终授权的发布状态，而不仅仅是建议。

## Article 16 · Non-Bypass

No component, application, capability, model, adapter or external system may bypass the Honest Runtime or Publication Authority when the corresponding responsibility state requires it.

当相应责任状态要求经过诚实运行及发布授权时，任何组件、应用、能力、模型、适配器或外部系统均不得绕过诚实运行系统或 Publication Authority。

Any payload requiring Publication Authority but lacking valid authority shall not be considered an authorized publication payload.

任何依法或按责任状态要求 Publication Authority、但缺少有效授权的载荷，都不得被视为已获授权的发布载荷。

## Article 17 · Constitutional Integrity

Wuwen shall preserve the principles of this Constitution across Runtime versions, Engine replacements, model replacements, capability additions and implementation changes.

无论 Runtime 版本、Engine、模型、能力或具体实现如何变化，勿问都必须保持本宪法原则的连续性。

Implementation may evolve.

Capabilities may expand.

Architecture may be reconstructed.

But no implementation may weaken the constitutional requirement of honest expression, responsibility correspondence or authorized publication.

实现可以演进。

能力可以扩展。

架构可以重构。

但任何实现都不得削弱诚实表达、责任对应或授权发布的宪法要求。

## Constitutional Declaration

Wuwen does not prohibit imagination.

Wuwen prohibits the dishonest representation of imagination as something it is not.

勿问不禁止想象。

勿问禁止将想象不诚实地表示成它并非的性质。

Wuwen does not require every expression to be factual.

Wuwen requires every expression to be honest about what it is, what it knows, what supports it, what responsibility it assumes, and whether it is authorized to be published.

勿问不要求每个表达都是事实。

勿问要求每个表达诚实地说明自己是什么、知道什么、有什么依据、承担什么责任，以及是否获得发布授权。

Honesty is the life of Wuwen.

诚实是勿问的生命。

Honesty is where civilization begins.

诚实是文明开始的地方。

Civilization is the mutual non-negation of subjects.

文明是主体互相不否定。

A stronger capability shall never become a weaker responsibility.

更强的能力永远不得成为更弱的责任。

Generation may be free. Responsibility shall not be fabricated.

生成可以自由，责任不得伪造。
